## 1. Primary evidence on adoption and failure of strategy-execution and OKR programs, and the effect of facilitation, executive sponsorship and cadence

### Takeaway
Better evidence now exists, but none of it tests "software alone" against "software plus facilitator" for strategy tools. The strongest primary evidence comes from randomized management-consulting experiments: intensive outside help changes practices, but about half of the new practices are dropped over the following years, and the main causes are manager turnover and lack of owner time. This directly supports the "service until rhythm sticks" conclusion and the risk that the review cadence dies.

### Cited Findings
- Randomized experiment, Indian weaving firms, revisited nine years after free intensive management consulting: roughly half of the introduced practices were abandoned, yet treated plants kept a significant gap in practices and performance versus controls. Practices spread within firms but rarely across firms. The two most cited reasons for dropping practices were managerial turnover and lack of director (owner) time. Published in American Economic Journal: Applied Economics 12(2), 2020, pp. 198-219 — [NBER w24249](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24249); [AEA DOI page](https://www.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/app.20180369); [VoxDev summary](https://www.voxdev.org/topic/firms/do-management-interventions-last-evidence-india) (search summary of the papers, not fetched in full).
- The same authors note that lighter consulting engagements in smaller firms led to firms gradually discarding the practices over the next three years (they cite other studies for this) — [NBER w24249 PDF](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24249.pdf) (search summary). The cited underlying studies were not identified or read.
- Peer-reviewed qualitative case study, Journal of Business Strategy 47(1), 2026 (online November 2025), pp. 52-70: one European financial-services firm, 11 semi-structured interviews in April 2023, OKR pilot with 14 product teams over three quarterly cycles (nine months). OKRs helped strategy implementation through four dimensions: facilitated communication, transparency, empowered employees, cross-functional collaboration. The rollout used certified OKR masters embedded in teams and one OKR champion who kept teams consistent. Cadence: quarterly cycle with planning, weekly self-monitoring of about 15 minutes inside existing team meetings, formal end-of-cycle review and retrospective. Typical failure: teams set OKRs and then forget them during the quarter. The author says success depends on cultural readiness and leadership commitment but did not measure leadership. The article cites an earlier study (Becker et al., 2022) listing barriers: no shared mindset, insufficient facilitation resources, need for clear communication, and time intensity — [Emerald, Journal of Business Strategy](https://www.emerald.com/jbs/article/47/1/52/1311605/Engaging-the-entire-organization-how-OKRs-enhance) (fetched; small sample, sample skewed to OKR practitioners).
- An evaluation of a large international bank's OKR rollout reports that key-result scores were not used for performance evaluation. Monthly performance dialogues discussed contribution and support instead — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404289086_Evaluating_a_large_international_bank's_OKR_rollout_using_universal_deployment_parameters) (search summary).
- An academic literature survey on OKR notes reported implementation difficulties and says OKR needs managers acting as facilitators and supporters; the ideal management style is contested — [ACM, Surveying the Academic Literature on the Use of OKR](https://dl.acm.org/doi/fullHtml/10.1145/3592813.3592934) (search summary).
- Prosci's own change-management research (vendor of change-management training): projects with an extremely effective sponsor met or exceeded objectives more than twice as often as those with a very ineffective sponsor. Only 48% of participants reported an effective or very effective sponsor. "Active and visible participation" was the sponsor activity cited most. Sponsors struggled most with building a sponsor coalition. Applying a structured method: 59% reached good or excellent change-management effectiveness versus 26% without. Dedicated resources also raised effectiveness — [Prosci, Primary sponsor's role](https://www.prosci.com/resources/articles/primary-sponsors-role-and-importance); [Prosci, Best practices](https://www.prosci.com/resources/articles/change-management-best-practices) (search summary, vendor; sample and edition not seen). The earlier "73% vs 29%" figure in the repository came secondhand from Mooncamp and was not found on a Prosci page in this pass.
- Facilitation evidence from healthcare implementation science is mixed. One VA study tested adding implementation facilitation to national technical assistance — [PMC4551776](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4551776/). The FIRE trial found no significant effect of facilitation on guideline compliance — [PMC6238352](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6238352/) (both search summary; different domain).
- Earlier repository evidence still stands: EIU/Brightline 2017 (20% of strategic objectives missed due to poor implementation; 59% struggle to bridge strategy and daily work) and PMI/EIU 2016 (61% of strategy-critical projects delivered intended benefits). Both are surveys of large firms — see `research_notes/Yazılım hizmet ve rol modeli/hizmet_ve_roller.md`, section A1.

### Inferences
- Inference: The randomized consulting evidence is the strongest available support for "software is not enough". It shows that outside help changes behavior, and that the change decays when the owner has no time or the responsible manager leaves. In Karar Atlası terms: karar sahibi time and stratej continuity are the two failure points.
- Inference: The decay evidence argues against ending all human support at week 8. A light recurring service (partner-run quarterly 3.5 review) is better supported than a one-off pilot.
- Inference: The OKR case suggests a concrete cadence pattern that fits the product: weekly 15-minute check inside an existing meeting (ekip üyesi gözlem), monthly decision (Karar 6/7), quarterly review (3.5). The weekly check should be embedded in a meeting the firm already holds, not a new meeting.
- Inference: The bank case supports the product rule that the system never scores employees. Scores were kept out of performance evaluation there.

### Gaps
- No study compares strategy software outcomes with and without a facilitator.
- The Mexico SME consulting randomized trial (Bruhn, Karlan and Schoar, 2018) was not retrieved in this session. It is relevant and should be read before citing.
- No Gartner, Forrester or TSIA primary page was read.
- No Turkish data on strategy-software or OKR adoption was found.

## 2. How strategy-execution vendors package services in 2026

### Takeaway
Vendors still hide service fees. The best new evidence is StrategyFrame, a German SME strategy SaaS: about 60% of its roughly 100 customers buy a six-month human coaching program on top of the software. Training and certification prices are published (WorkBoard 599 USD); implementation fees and partner commissions are not.

### Cited Findings
- StrategyFrame (Düsseldorf, founded 2022): about 100 Mittelstand companies use its AI strategy platform. About 40% use only the basic SaaS package and run their strategy themselves. The rest add a coaching program with up to two workshops and at most three days per month over six months. No prices are published. The article does not say whether coaches are staff or partners — [The Innovator, Startup of the Week](https://theinnovator.news/?p=11868) (fetched; article undated, likely late 2025 by image dates; founder-sourced). A self-paced Results Foundry course is 119 USD. A Level 2 certification targets coaches who have coached teams through 2 or more quarterly OKR cycles. Listed session dates seen were from 2024, so current 2026 dates are unconfirmed — [WorkBoard OKR Coach Certification](https://learn.workboard.com/page/okr-coach-certification); [Level 2](https://learn.workboard.com/page/advanced-okr-coach-certification); [Results Foundry](https://www.workboard.com/okr-forum) (search summary, vendor).
- Cascade (vendor): partner page names four partner types: strategy consulting firms, technology providers, training and development organizations, independent consultants. Benefits named: expand service offering, reach new markets, deepen client engagement, co-innovation, revenue through referral programs, joint service offerings and co-marketing. - Spider Strategies, Spider Impact (vendor): partners get web-based training, demo tools and a dedicated trial environment for prospects. ### Inferences
- Inference: StrategyFrame is the closest analog to Karar Atlası (SME, strategy formulation, not only OKR tracking). Its 60/40 split is founder-reported, but it suggests most SME buyers pay for human help even when a self-serve tier exists.
- Inference: StrategyFrame's coaching intensity (max 3 days per month for 6 months, at most 2 workshops) is larger than an 8-week pilot. Karar Atlası's pilot plus a quarterly partner-run review is a lighter but longer pattern.
- Inference: Every vendor reviewed keeps training and certification as named, priced products, but keeps implementation fees and partner economics private. Karar Atlası would stand out by publishing a fixed pilot price.

### Gaps

## 3. Customer success benchmarks for mid-market B2B SaaS

### Takeaway
Benchmarks are mostly vendor-published and inconsistent. The usable anchors: target time to first value under 30 days, mid-market onboarding of about 30 to 60 days, and CSM coverage of about 22 to 49 accounts for 10K to 100K USD contracts and about 144 accounts below 10K USD. Karar Atlası's planned price (about 300 USD per month, about 3,600 USD per year) sits in the low-touch band, which conflicts with the planned 2 hours of support per customer per month.

### Cited Findings
- Gainsight analysis of its US customers' data (vendor; blog dated 29 July 2022): high touch (above 100K USD ACV) about 22 accounts per CSM; mid touch (10K to 100K USD ACV) about 49; low touch (below 10K USD ACV) about 144. ARR per CSM: median 1.4M USD, top quartile 4.2M USD, based on 17,034 CSMs. The post does not link coverage to retention — [Gainsight, right CSM to customer ratio](https://www.gainsight.com/blog/gainsight-horizon-ai-labs-what-is-the-right-csm-to-customer-ratio/) (fetched).
- A third-party summary of Gainsight's CS Index puts mid-market at about 35 accounts per CSM and 1.6M USD ARR per CSM, SMB at 110 accounts and 1.2M USD — [successcoaching.co](https://cfo.successcoaching.co/docs/focus-cs-coverage) (search summary; secondary, not confirmed against the Gainsight report). Gainsight's 2025 CS Index page says its customers cover about 25% more accounts per CSM than non-customers in commercial and enterprise segments — [Gainsight 2025 CS Index](https://www.gainsight.com/blog/new-cs-index-report-reveals-trends-to-watch-in-2025/) (search summary, vendor).
- Time to value: Userpilot's 2025 benchmark across 62 B2B SaaS companies gives an average of about 1.5 days from signup to first value (product-led tools) — [shno.co aggregation](https://www.shno.co/marketing-statistics/saas-onboarding-statistics) (search summary, secondary). A 2026 vendor report gives a 9-day median TTV for 25K to 100K USD ARR accounts — [Perspective AI](https://getperspective.ai/blog/2026-customer-onboarding-benchmark-activation-rates-by-industry) (search summary, vendor). Other blogs give mid-market onboarding completion of 30 to 60 days and enterprise of 60 to 90 days — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/post-sale-management/onboarding-metrics) (search summary).
- A venture investor's customer success guide suggests a target time to value under 30 days and gives 10% to 20% annual logo churn as a rough mid-market planning range — [Tomasz Tunguz guide](https://tomtunguz.com/customer-success-guide/index.md) (search summary).
- A claim that 30% to 50% of churn happens in the first 90 days appears without a traceable primary source — [amworldgroup](https://amworldgroup.com/statistics/customer-onboarding-statistics) (search summary; treat as unverified).
- Health score inputs in a leading CS platform: engagement, product usage, support history and loyalty metrics; playbooks trigger on falling usage or approaching renewal — [Cuspera on ChurnZero](https://www.cuspera.com/products/churnzero-x-7977) (search summary).

### Inferences
- Inference: "First value" for Karar Atlası is not a login event. It is the karar sahibi's first recorded decision (Karar 1 or the geçiş kararı). A 30-day target for the first recorded decision is consistent with the benchmarks and fits inside the 8-week pilot.
- Inference: Product-led TTV figures (1.5 days) do not apply. This product is a facilitated, decision-centred process; the right comparison is mid-market onboarding of 30 to 60 days.
- Inference: Coverage arithmetic: at 2 support hours per customer per month and about 140 productive hours per person per month, one person covers about 70 customers. The low-touch benchmark for a 3,600 USD ACV is about 144 accounts. So either support hours must fall to about 1 hour per month after the pilot, or partners must carry part of the touch. This matches the "partner carries facilitation" model.

### Gaps
- No TSIA or ChurnZero primary benchmark report was read.
- No renewal-driver study specific to strategy or planning tools was found.

- Inference: The mali müşavir is already in the persona work as a Danışman (dış göz). Accountants are a second partner channel to test, with a lighter certification than strategy consultants.

## 5. Turkish strategy and kurumsallaşma consulting practice, and 2026 public support rules

### Takeaway
Turkish strategy consultancies describe a multi-phase engagement with a 1 to 2 day workshop and no fixed length; some sources cite 4 to 8 weeks. No public prices were found. KOSGEB has no 2026 grant line for strategy or kurumsallaşma consulting in the directive read; its closest line pays 80% of a TÜSSİDE-certified digital transformation roadmap up to 20,000 TL. KOSGEB's capacity development loan program and development agency technical support can carry consulting costs, mostly indirectly.

### Cited Findings
- MirAdvisory (vendor, Turkish consultancy): six phases: analysis (SWOT, competitors, PESTLE; output situation report), strategy workshop (agreed priorities), goals and KPIs (KPI card), strategies and action plan (roadmap with owners, budget, timeline), implementation and monitoring (dashboard and management rhythm), evaluation and revision (updated plan). Workshops usually last 1 to 2 days, up to a week for large transformations. The timeline is agreed per project. No price shown. Target clients include boards preparing for generational transition — [MirAdvisory strateji](https://miradvisory.com/tr/hizmetler/strateji) (fetched).

- Earlier repository finding: KOSGEB digital transformation credit does not cover consulting — [erp-software.org](https://erp-software.org/en/erp-worldwide/turkey/), cited in `reports/Yazılım hizmet ve rol modeli.md`.

### Inferences
- Inference: An 8-week single-decision pilot with three fixed outputs is shorter and narrower than a typical Turkish strategy engagement (multi-phase, open timeline). It sits near the 4 to 8 week figure some consultancies give. This makes the pilot easy to explain as "the first part of a strategy project, with software".
- Inference: The KOSGEB YÖNDE limits are small (20,000 TL per roadmap service). They signal how the state prices a short, structured, certified-consultant diagnostic. A pilot priced far above this will not look like a "subsidizable diagnostic" to SMEs. A pilot priced near a few multiples of it, plus subscription, is more defensible.
- Inference: The KOSGEB YÖNDE model is a Turkish precedent for a certified-consultant registry: certification by an external body (TÜSSİDE), work logged in a portal, audits, and removal from the database. A Karar Atlası partner registry could copy this shape: certification, work recorded in the product, quality review, removal.
- Inference: Kapasite Geliştirme lets eligible fast-growing manufacturing or IT SMEs finance software and consultancy inside a growth project. A partner could place the subscription plus pilot inside such a project. This applies only to eligible sectors.
- Inference: Development agency TD funds usually go to institutions (chambers, associations), as the Samsun TSO case shows. A chamber-sponsored cohort (for example 10 to 30 member family firms) is a plausible channel for partner-run pilots.

### Gaps
- No public Turkish price for strategy or kurumsallaşma consulting was found. Armut lists consultants as quote-only (earlier notes).
- No 2026 KOSGEB grant line explicitly for kurumsallaşma, aile anayasası or strategic planning consulting was confirmed.
- The eligibility of individual firms for development agency TD in 2026 was not confirmed from a full guide.

## 6. Training and certification models for facilitators, and what a Karar Atlası partner certification could require

### Takeaway
Credible certifications combine training hours, proof of practice (a number of facilitated sessions or completed cycles), an assessment and renewal. WorkBoard's two levels (599 USD course; Level 2 needs 2 or more coached quarterly cycles) are the closest product-tied model. IAF requires 7 workshops in 3 years plus a two-stage peer assessment. The Balanced Scorecard Institute uses multi-day courses with exam.

### Cited Findings
- IAF Certified Professional Facilitator (CPF): requires IAF membership, facilitation of at least 7 workshops in the last 3 years, evidence of core competencies, and a two-stage peer assessment (written submission, then a live assessment day). Fees conflict across IAF pages: 500 USD Stage 1 plus 2,000 USD Stage 2; a single 2,000 USD assessment fee; or an older single 2,500 USD fee. An annual fee of 500 USD and an appeal fee of 850 or 1,600 USD appear on different pages. Validity is given as 3 years on one page and 4 years on another — [IAF certification fee policies](https://iaf-world.org/iaf-certification-fee-policies/); [IAF PD fees list 12 March 2026](https://iaf-world.org/pd-fees-list-and-policy-20260312/); [IAF career page](https://iaf-world.org/your-career/) (search summary).
- Balanced Scorecard Institute: Balanced Scorecard Professional (BSP) is a 5-day program: Course 301 (3 days, 2,700 USD) and Course 401 (2 days, 1,800 USD). Master Professional (BSMP) via a 10-day course plus exam, or BSP plus a 5-day application course (Course 520, 4,500 USD, plus 900 USD for CEUs). Third-party 5-day boot camps list 3,900 to 5,945 USD — [BSI Professional certification](https://balancedscorecard.org/training-certification/balanced-scorecard-professional-certification); [BSI Master Professional](https://balancedscorecard.org/training-certification/balanced-scorecard-master-professional-certification/) (search summary, vendor). This is the BSI program, not a Kaplan-Norton branded credential.

### Inferences
- Inference: A Karar Atlası partner certification could have two levels, mirroring WorkBoard and IAF:
  1. Level 1, "Sertifikalı Stratej": short course (about 2 days or 4 half days), covering the product rules (stratej proposes, karar sahibi chooses and records; AI never decides; no employee scoring), the 2.4 selection meeting script, KVKK roles, and the three pilot outputs. Exam: run a demo plan to a geçiş kararı.
  2. Level 2, "Kıdemli Stratej": proof of practice, for example 2 completed pilots with all three outputs recorded and at least 2 quarterly 3.5 reviews run, plus a reviewed recording or observation of one 2.4 meeting.
  3. Renewal every year or two, tied to active plans (the product already records whether reviews happened on time).
  4. Delisting rule, like KOSGEB's: a partner whose plans repeatedly miss reviews loses the directory listing.
- Inference: Because the product logs decisions and reviews, proof of practice can be verified from product data, not self-report. This is stronger than IAF's portfolio.
- Inference: Price anchors: 599 USD (WorkBoard) to about 2,700 USD (BSI 3-day course). A Turkish price was not researched.

## 7. Usage cadence evidence: which usage signals predict renewal in planning tools

### Takeaway
No public data was found on renewal predictors for strategy or planning tools. Generic SaaS sources point to falling active use and sponsor change. The randomized management study gives indirect primary evidence: practices decay when the responsible manager leaves or the owner lacks time.

### Cited Findings
- Consultant and vendor blogs cite falling active users as the most consistent churn predictor, for example a 20% drop over three weeks as a 45 to 60 day warning with 78% accuracy, but with no traceable primary source — [PulseRevOps](https://pulserevops.com/knowledge/q196) (search summary; unverified).
- A vendor argues sponsor changes are invisible to product telemetry and must be captured through conversation — [Perspective AI](https://getperspective.ai/blog/at-risk-customer-identification-the-conversational-signals-that-beat-usage-data-alone) (search summary, vendor opinion).
- Claims attributed to ChurnZero 2026 studies (73% of CS leaders say health scores fail to predict churn; login decline precedes 81% of voluntary churn) appear only on a consultant site; the studies were not found (search summary; unverified).
- Managerial turnover and lack of owner time were the most cited reasons firms dropped new management practices — [NBER w24249](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24249) (search summary).
- The OKR case study names "set and forget during the quarter" as the typical failure — [Emerald](https://www.emerald.com/jbs/article/47/1/52/1311605/Engaging-the-entire-organization-how-OKRs-enhance) (fetched).

### Inferences
- Inference: The repository's plan-level health signals are consistent with this evidence and should stay as hypotheses: missed 3.5 review, no observation for 30 days, no karar sahibi record for 30 days, stratej or danışman membership ended without replacement.
- Inference: Add one signal the evidence suggests: a change of karar sahibi (a new time-bounded assignment) should trigger a partner call, because owner or manager change is the strongest decay cause in the primary study.
- Inference: Keep signals plan-level, not person-level, in line with the product rule against scoring employees and the bank OKR case.

### Gaps
- No public renewal-predictor data for Cascade, ClearPoint, WorkBoard, AchieveIt or similar tools was found.
- No study tested whether a partner-run quarterly review raises renewal.
