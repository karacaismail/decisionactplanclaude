# Reach channels to Turkish mid-size family firms, and sizing of the secondary segments (founders, consultants, large groups, public sector), as of 16 November 2026

Scope note. Research done on 16 November 2026 (Monday) with web search and fetch, about 50 tool calls. Product context: Karar Atlası, a Turkey-origin strategy-planning SaaS. Primary paying ICP is Turkish family-owned firms with 50 to 499 employees. Entry tier is solo founders and small teams. Consultants are the channel. Large companies and holdings are the second wave. Public institutions (universities first) are a later, separate offer.

Source-status labels:
- **[PRIMARY-READ]** = I read the original document or the institution's own page in this session (for PDFs, text was extracted locally).
- **[SEARCH-SNIPPET]** = the figure came from a search-engine summary of the page; I did not open the page. The writer should treat the exact wording as unverified.
- **[SECONDARY]** = a press or third-party page reporting someone else's figure.
- **[OLD]** = the figure is older than 2024 or undated.

Many institutional sites (sp.gov.tr, istatistik.yok.gov.tr, iso.org.tr subpages) failed to load or rendered only JavaScript. Several key counts therefore come from press reports. Conflicts are shown side by side. No figure below was estimated by me unless it is marked as an inference.

## 1. Channels to mid-size family firms: chambers, TOBB, exporter unions, OSBs, business associations, KalDer, banks, fairs, LinkedIn, business press

### Takeaway
The densest, most countable route to 50 to 499 employee family firms runs through industrial structures: about 418 organized industrial zones (OSBs) with about 68,000 to 69,000 firms, the sanayi odaları (İSO alone reports 22,000+ members), the İSO 500 and İkinci 500 lists, and TİM's İlk 1000 exporters list. Strategy, kurumsallaşma and succession content already flows through chamber academies (BTSO Akademi), bank family-business academies (Akbank, ING), TAİDER and KalDer's EFQM track. These are partner and event channels, not ad channels. Exact membership counts for many bodies (KSO, KAYSO, KAGİDER, TÜGİAD, TİM's total member base) were not found.

### Inferences
- OSBs are the best physical channel. About 68,000 firms sit in about 300 operating zones, each with a management office. A few OSB pilots (Bursa, Kayseri, Gaziantep, Manisa, Kocaeli) would reach many 50 to 499 employee manufacturers at once.
- The İSO 500, İkinci 500 and TİM İlk 1000 lists give named, public target lists. Firms ranked roughly 500 to 1,000 in İSO lists and most of the İlk 1000 exporters are likely near the top of the ICP size band or above it. The ICP's 50 to 499 band sits mostly below these lists. So the lists are better for the second wave and for "reference" logos than for volume.
- Chamber academies (BTSO Akademi) and bank academies (Akbank, ING) already teach "stratejik yönetim", "iş planı" and "kurumsallaşma" to family firms. A co-branded module where participants build one real decision in the product is a natural entry. The bank programs are also budget holders for SME content.
- TAİDER and KalDer are small but high-intent: TAİDER members are self-selected family firms working on succession; KalDer members are already doing EFQM self-assessment, which needs strategy-to-results evidence.
- Trade fairs give volume (about 65,000 exhibitors in 2025) but low intent for strategy software. They suit consultant partners more than direct sales.

### Inferences
- Municipal plans largely run 2025–2029. The next mandatory municipal plan wave follows the next local elections, expected in 2029 (inference from the six-month rule; the election date was not verified). In 2026 to 2028, municipalities are in monitoring and annual-evaluation mode, not plan-writing mode. A municipal offer for this window would be monitoring and evaluation, not plan preparation.
- Universities are the open window now. Several are drafting 2027–2031 plans in 2026, and others will start 2028–2032 drafts in 2027. Universities also have strateji daire başkanlıkları and a common rehber, which favors one standard package.
- A first university license priced below the 2026 doğrudan temin limit (340,391 TL outside metropolitan areas, 1,021,827 TL inside) avoids a tender. This is an inference; each institution's internal procurement rules still apply.
- The product should export into SBB formats and not replace programbutce.sbb.gov.tr, which is mandatory for performance programs.

## 6. Summary per channel and segment: size, reachability, cost signals, tie to the primary ICP

### Takeaway
For the primary ICP, the highest-yield routes are partner channels with built-in trust: OSB management offices, sanayi odaları academies, bank family-business academies, TAİDER and KalDer, plus strategy and kurumsallaşma consultants. Founders and public institutions are separate funnels. Founders are reachable cheaply through KOSGEB and technopark programs, while universities have a concrete 2026–2028 plan window and a tender-free purchase band.
