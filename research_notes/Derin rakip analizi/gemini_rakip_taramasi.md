## 1. New Competitors & Adjacent Substitutes (30)

### Cluster Map (Reference for Overlap Column)
* **C1 (Pre-Strategy & Readiness):** Evidence-based readiness gate and pre-strategy preparation.
* **C2 (Formulation & Decision Record):** Diagnosis, options, testing, explicit trade-offs, key moves, recorded decision + rationale.
* **C3 (Execution & Governance):** Targets, initiatives, resource permits/spend tracking, periodic reviews (+ offline PWA/mobile).
