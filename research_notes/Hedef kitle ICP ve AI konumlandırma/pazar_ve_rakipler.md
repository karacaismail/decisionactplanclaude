# Strategy planning and strategy execution software: market, competitors, buyers, pricing, AI (state as of mid-November 2026)

Research note scope: commercial vendors of strategy management / strategy execution / OKR software, plus founder business-plan tools; market size; buyer roles; reviews; formulation gap; Turkey. Research date: 15 November 2026. Many vendor pricing pages hide prices ("contact us"); where a number comes from an aggregator (Capterra, G2, Software Advice, ITQlick, Vendr, Tability) it is marked as such. Aggregator data can be stale.

## Q1. Main vendors: segment, list price, pricing model, positioning, AI features

### Takeaway
Enterprise and mid-market strategy-execution vendors (Cascade, WorkBoard incl. ex-Quantive, ClearPoint, Spider Impact, AchieveIt, Envisio, Profit.co, Jira Align) almost all hide prices behind quotes; only OKR tools aimed at SMB/mid-market (Perdoo, Mooncamp, low tiers of Quantive) publish per-seat prices around EUR 6-10 per user per month. Founder business-plan tools (LivePlan, Upmetrics, ProjectionHub) publish cheap self-serve prices (USD 15-49 per month). The biggest 2025 structural events were WorkBoard acquiring Quantive (May 2025) and Microsoft retiring Viva Goals (31 December 2025); in 2026 the AI story is "agents" (Chief of Staff, Portfolio Analyst) and MCP integrations, mostly on the execution/reporting side.

### Inferences
- Pricing bifurcation: (a) self-serve OKR tools at EUR 6-10/seat/month for SMB; (b) quote-only "platform pricing" for strategy execution suites, with real enterprise contracts in the USD 25k-150k/year range (WorkBoard median about USD 57k per Vendr; Cascade about USD 25k for 50 users per estimate). A mid-market gap in transparent pricing between these exists.
- Seat model is shifting: Cascade prices by "platform" with limited executive seats and unlimited viewers; Perdoo sells cheap view-only seats; WorkBoard prices agents by usage; Perdoo meters AI by tokens. A new entrant can expect buyers to compare against "unlimited viewers" plus metered AI.
- AI positioning in 2026 is concentrated on execution-side automation (status updates, reports, MBR/QBR packs, risk flags, scenario modelling) and on MCP/LLM connectors, not on structured strategy formulation.
- Consolidation (Quantive into WorkBoard; Viva Goals exit) created a pool of migrating customers in 2025-2026.

## Q4. What reviews say: loves and hates

### Takeaway
Review-site ratings are high (4.6-4.8 on G2) and praise ease of use and support; recurring complaints are missing features and, more strongly in practitioner and vendor-blog sources, adoption decay, update burden and stale data. Strong evidence that tools do not solve adoption on their own.

### Cited Findings
- Cascade on G2: pros most cited "Ease of Use" (23 mentions) and "Customer Support" (17); cons "Missing Features" (7) and "Feature Issues" (3) — [G2 compare Cascade vs Quantive](https://www.g2.com/compare/cascade-strategy-cascade-vs-quantive-strategyai)

- Profit.co enterprise study: more than half of enterprise OKR programs are considerably reduced or abandoned after year one — [Profit.co](https://www.profit.co/blog/okr-university/why-most-enterprise-okr-programs-fail-in-year-two/)

## Q5. Is there a gap in formulation, choice and pre-strategy readiness?

### Takeaway
Most vendors start at "you already have a strategy; now cascade, track and report it" (ClearPoint states this explicitly). Formulation claims exist (Quantive StrategyAI 2024 "making strategic choices"; WorkBoard Portfolio Analyst scenario modelling; small players like StrategyFrame.AI), but no vendor found offers a structured diagnosis to options to testing to choice-with-trade-offs workflow plus a pre-strategy evidence/readiness gate. That remains an apparent gap, with general-purpose LLMs and consultants filling it.

### Cited Findings
- ClearPoint's target is organizations "that already have goals or a strategy" — [ClearPoint pricing](https://www.clearpointstrategy.com/pricing)
- Cascade Planner positions as moving strategy "out of static sheets into a living system" with AI flagging duplications, dependencies, risks (execution/planning structure, not choice-making) — [Cascade Planner](https://cascade.app/features/planner)
- Quantive StrategyAI (Feb 2024) claimed full-cycle coverage incl. making strategic choices; product now absorbed into WorkBoard — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2024/02/29/quantive-unveils-ai-driven-strategy-platform-enhance-business-decision-making/); [Tability](https://www.tability.io/compare/vs/okrstool-vs-quantive)
- WorkBoard Portfolio Analyst agent models strategy scenarios in real time (portfolio level) — [TechIntelPro](https://techintelpro.com/news/ai/enterprise-ai/workboard-launches-ai-native-strategic-portfolio-management-with-portfolio-analyst-agents)
- StrategyFrame.AI (Germany, founded 2022): AI strategy system for SMEs/Mittelstand doing their own strategy work instead of hiring consultants; about 100 companies; plans US expansion in 2026 — [The Innovator](https://theinnovator.news/?p=11868)
- Cascade's own roundup lists Cascade, WorkBoard, Wave by McKinsey, ClearPoint, Profit.co as "AI strategy platforms" and argues general LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) play a different role (self-promotional source) — [Cascade blog](https://cascade.app/blog/best-ai-strategy-platforms)
- HBR (per trade article) notes AI-native boutique consulting startups selling strategy as AI-enabled service — [Upmetrics blog / search summary](https://upmetrics.co/blog/ai-strategy-tools) (weak source)
- Founder tools (LivePlan, Upmetrics) cover business-plan writing and forecasts with AI drafting, not organizational strategy choice — [LivePlan pricing](https://www.liveplan.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- Gap hypothesis supported: formulation (diagnosis, options, testing, explicit trade-offs) and readiness gating are poorly productized; execution tools assume a strategy exists; founder tools produce documents, not decisions.
- Risk: incumbents with AI agents could extend upstream; WorkBoard's scenario modelling and Quantive's earlier "choices" messaging show intent.
- Readiness (evidence, data quality, leadership alignment before strategy) has no identified dedicated product; closest analogue is consultant-led discovery.

### Gaps
- Did not check Strategyzer, Shibumi, Wave by McKinsey, Jibility or other formulation-adjacent tools in depth.
- No found product explicitly marketing a "readiness gate" before strategy work.

## Q6. Turkey: local vendors, consulting-led work, public-sector planning under Law 5018

### Takeaway
The Turkish public sector has a legal obligation for strategic plans and performance programmes (Law 5018) and central-government institutions prepare performance programmes in the Presidency of Strategy and Budget's own web system (programbutce.sbb.gov.tr), which limits commercial software room for central bodies. Municipalities and universities have planning, monitoring and evaluation duties, and academic studies say monitoring/evaluation systems remain weak. Only one Turkish OKR startup (Twiser/Wokr, 2021 data) surfaced; no Turkish commercial strategy-formulation product found.

### Cited Findings
- Performance programme preparation for central administrations runs in the SBB system programbutce.sbb.gov.tr; the process starts by end of May each year with a written notice from the top executive; institutions without a strategic plan are not obliged to prepare a performance programme — [SBB 2027-2029 guide, Section 7 (September 2026)](https://www.sbb.gov.tr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2027-2029Rehber_Bolum7.pdf)
- Law 5018 Article 9 defines strategic plan and performance programme obligations — [Alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2021/04/22/kamu-stratejik-planlar-ve-performans-programlari/)
- Municipalities with population over 50,000 must prepare strategic plans; annual performance programmes are approved by the municipal council — [MEB strategic planning guide](https://bayrakli.meb.gov.tr/etkinlikler/Sp_Klavuz.pdf)

### Inferences
- Central government performance programme data entry is captured by SBB's system, so a product for public institutions must complement it (preparation, evidence, monitoring, internal reviews, export to SBB format) rather than replace it.
- Municipalities (50,000+ population), universities and other local/special-budget bodies with weak monitoring/evaluation are a plausible segment, but procurement rules (public tender, local data hosting) apply.
- Turkish private-sector strategy work appears consulting-led; no evidence of a strong local SaaS competitor in formulation.

### Gaps
- No Turkish commercial vendor of public-sector strategic plan software was identified (likely exist as local e-government/ERP module vendors; requires targeted search of EKAP tender records and vendor sites).
- No data on Turkish consulting firms' strategy engagement prices or volumes.
- Twiser/Wokr 2026 status, pricing and customer count unverified.
- No Turkish market size for OKR/strategy software.
