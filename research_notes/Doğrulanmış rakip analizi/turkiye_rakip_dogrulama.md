# Turkish competitors, public tenders and substitutes: second verification round (as of 16 Kasım 2026 Pazartesi)

Scope: every flagged Turkish, public-sector and substitute item in `reports/Derin rakip analizi.md` (sections 5.9, 6.1 to 6.6, 7.3, 9, 12). Codex and Gemini notes were treated as untrusted leads. All pages below were downloaded on 16 Kasım 2026 Pazartesi into an isolated folder and parsed locally. "Page" means the PDF page number unless the document's printed number is given. No CAPTCHA or login was bypassed. No private individuals are named.

Status words used in the table:

- **Doğrulandı**: the primary source says it.
- **Düzeltildi**: the primary source says something different.
- **Kısmen**: part is primary, part stays secondary.
- **Erişilemedi**: every route listed was tried; the best substitute answer is given.

## Q1. Public tenders and public buyers

### Takeaway
No dedicated "strategic plan software" award amount could be confirmed from a primary source. But primary buyer documents now prove who uses what: Osmaniye lists Dijitek and Bimser SPNET, Gebze lists Bimser, Altınordu bought a "Stratejik Plan" software item in 2025, and Ege runs its own system. Three report claims are wrong: Efeler's 5,500,000 TL is a GIS and ERP target, Mersin is a research-project purchase, and Zeytinburnu's "similar work" is public-relations work.

### Inferences
- Public buyers rarely buy "strategic plan software" as a large tender. Primary documents show small software lines (Altınordu), ERP bundles (Efeler, Altınordu SAMPAŞ), vendor modules (Gebze, Osmaniye) and in-house systems (Ege, KSÜ).
- Dijitek moves from "tek kaynak, sayfa açılmadı" to a vendor with at least one named municipal user (Osmaniye).

### Gaps
- No primary award amount for any strategic-plan software. Only a person with an EKAP account can search awards and KİK decisions.
