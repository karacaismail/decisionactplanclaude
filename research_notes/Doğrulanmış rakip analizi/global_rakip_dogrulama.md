# Global competitor verification, second round (Karar Atlası)

Scope: global items in `reports/Derin rakip analizi.md` (sections 5, 7, 8, 9), with priority on items marked "tek kaynak" and items that move the white-space claim or the "most dangerous" ranking. Codex and Gemini notes were treated as untrusted leads. Every fact below was re-read on a vendor page, a vendor press release, a vendor price list, or an archived snapshot. All pages were seen on 16 November 2026 unless a snapshot date is given. No private individuals are named. Turkish "corrected wording" cells follow the plain-Turkish rules of the project (short sentences, no semicolons, dates with day names).

Status words used in the table:

- **doğrulandı**: the primary source says the same thing.
- **düzeltildi**: the primary source says something different. The corrected wording replaces the old one.
- **kısmen doğrulandı**: part of the claim holds. The cell says which part.
- **erişilemedi**: every route failed. The routes are listed and the best substitute is given.

Routes used when a page was blocked: direct WebFetch, direct curl, Wayback Machine snapshot (`id_` raw capture), vendor help pages, vendor structured data (schema.org JSON in page source), vendor PDF price list, trade-press republications of press releases, review-site snapshots.

## Verification table for flagged global items

### Takeaway
Of 67 checked items, 41 were confirmed (some with added detail or a small fix), 16 were corrected, 7 were partly confirmed, 1 stays in conflict and 2 were unreachable but given a substitute source. The corrections that matter most are these. Smartsheet vsActual now has a general-availability target of early 2027, its own option-and-trade-off view and a "gap flag" for goals without targets. Success.co really advertises offline mode on all plans. BSC Designer's Turkish interface dates from April 2025 and it now has MCP. StrategyFrame.AI hosts in Germany or the EU and its entry price was reported as 15,000 euros a year. WorkBoard's "55%" figure is from September 2025, not June 2026.

## White space and ranking, judged with strategy research

### Takeaway
Research on decision quality supports a process gate before choice, and no checked vendor sells an evidence-based gate. The white space is narrower on "choice record" and "offline", but intact on "evidence gate plus Turkish plus Turkish data". The ranking should add BSC Designer as a named threat and note vsActual's 2027 date.

### Cited Findings
- Lovallo and Sibony studied 1,048 major business decisions over five years. They found that "process mattered more than analysis—by a factor of six" — [Farnam Street summary of McKinsey Quarterly](https://fs.blog/what-matters-more-in-decisions-analysis-or-process/). The McKinsey page timed out twice and had no Wayback snapshot, so the secondary summary is used.
- Kahneman, Lovallo and Sibony proposed a 12-question checklist that decision makers apply before approving a proposal. Citation: Harvard Business Review, June 2011, volume 89, issue 6, pages 50 to 60 — [PubMed record via NCBI E-utilities](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21714386/)
- Jarzabkowski and Kaplan argue that strategy tools matter through how practitioners use them ("tools-in-use"), not only through the tool's design. Strategic Management Journal 36(4), 2015, pages 537 to 558 — [EconPapers record](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:stratm:v:36:y:2015:i:4:p:537-558), [City Research Online](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/4011/)
- Rumelt's "kernel" puts diagnosis first, then a guiding policy, then coherent actions. Summaries note diagnosis is the step most often skipped — [Perdoo book review](https://www.perdoo.com/resources/blog/book-review-good-strategy-bad-strategy-by-richard-rumelt) (secondary)
- vsActual's own design enforces a single plan of record and human approval, and Futurum calls it "opinionated" — [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/?p=97541)

### Inferences
- Gate rationale. Lovallo and Sibony's process finding and the Kahneman checklist both argue for a step that checks the quality of the case before the choice. Rumelt's diagnosis-first kernel argues the same from strategy content. No checked vendor turns this into a blocking step tied to evidence strength. vsActual blocks incomplete plans, BSC Designer scores assessments, but neither blocks a choice for weak evidence. This is the strongest remaining white space and has academic backing.
- Adoption risk. Jarzabkowski and Kaplan's tools-in-use view, plus the review data on learning curves, suggests that a gate adds friction. Karar Atlası should show the gate as guidance with a clear reason, not as a hard wall without help. This is an inference.
- Ranking changes suggested:
  1. Keep "Danışman ile genel yapay zekâ" first.
  2. Keep StrategyFrame.AI fifth, but add its €15,000 per year entry figure (July 2025) and EU hosting.
  3. Rename group 6 to "Ucuz İngilizce ve Türkçe arayüzlü global araçlar". Add BSC Designer by name, because it has a Turkish UI, AI, MCP, budgets, evidence attachments and free viewers.
  4. Add Success.co to group 6 as the offline benchmark.
  5. Keep vsActual seventh, but write "genel satış 2027 başında planlanıyor".
- White-space parts in section 8, revised:
  1. Evidence-ladder readiness gate: still open. Nearest are vsActual gap flags and BSC Designer weighted assessments.
  2. Turkish plus Turkish data plus strategy formulation: still open for private family firms. BSC Designer covers Turkish UI but not Turkish data location or formulation from scratch.
  3. Resource permission tied to the chosen move: narrowed for large firms (vsActual, TransparentChoice, Decision Lens). Open for mid-size Turkish firms.
  4. Choice record that names the rejected option and the owner: narrowed. vsActual, StratNavApp and BSC Designer record decisions. None documents a mandatory "vazgeçilen seçenek ve gerekçe" field.
  5. Selling a single decision: TransparentChoice Sprint and 1000minds per-audit pricing show the model. A Turkish version is still open.

### Gaps
