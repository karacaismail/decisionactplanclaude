Research date: 15 November 2026. Scope: this note extends `research_notes/Hedef kitle ICP ve AI konumlandırma/pazar_ve_rakipler.md` (called "earlier note" below). It does not repeat what the earlier note already covers. The earlier note already has: Cascade, WorkBoard and Quantive, ClearPoint, Spider Impact, AchieveIt, Envisio, Profit.co, Perdoo, Mooncamp, Jira Align, LivePlan, Upmetrics, ProjectionHub, Bizplan, the Viva Goals retirement, market sizes, review sentiment, SBB's programbutce system and Twiser's 2021 Webrazzi profile. Read both notes together.

Conventions: "UNVERIFIED" means the claim comes from an aggregator, a search-result summary or the vendor's own marketing without an independent check. "Not found" means the item was searched for and no source was found. Prices are list prices or aggregator estimates on the date given. Product clusters used below: (1) pre-strategy readiness (evidence, readiness gate), (2) formulation (diagnosis, options), (3) choice and decision log (trade-offs, key moves), (4) execution tracking (targets, initiatives, reviews), (5) resource and budget control (resource permits), (6) mobile/offline, (7) Turkish language, (8) data in Turkey.

## Q1. Global categories and players: gaps filled beyond the earlier note

### Takeaway
Seven global categories touch the product, but none covers the full chain from readiness to choice to resource-controlled execution. Execution suites and OKR tools start after the strategy exists. Canvas tools (Strategyzer, Miro, Mural) help with thinking but keep no decision record. Decision tools (Cloverpop, Decision Lens, 1000minds) are enterprise or government prioritisation engines with no strategy method. EPM vendors (Anaplan, Board) are moving into "agentic" finance planning. The closest functional analogue is StrategyFrame.AI (Germany): mid-sized firms of 50 to 5,000 staff, analysis to options to team decision to OKRs, with coaches. It has no visible Turkey presence. Generic AI assistants are the cheapest substitute for formulation work.

### Inferences
- The global category is split by step. Nobody sells "readiness gate, then choice with a decision log, then resource-permitted execution" as one product. StrategyFrame.AI comes closest on formulation plus OKRs for mid-sized firms, but it is sold as a coached service, not a self-run product. It shows no Turkish language or Turkey presence.
- Decision tools (Cloverpop, Decision Lens) prove that buyers pay for decision records and prioritisation. They are enterprise or government products with US references, so they are not a direct threat to a Turkish family firm with 50 to 499 staff.
- EPM agent pushes (Anaplan, Board) target the CFO office and resource allocation. This could reach the "resource and budget control" cluster at large holdings, not at the first ICP.
- Generic AI assistants cost about 499 TL per month per person (ChatGPT Plus in Turkey). Copilot may already be inside the firm's Microsoft 365 contract. This makes them the most likely substitute for formulation work.

## Q2. Turkey: local vendors of strategy management, kurumsal performans yönetimi, BSC, OKR and stratejik plan izleme software

### Takeaway
Turkey has a crowded local market, but it splits into four groups, and none of them does formulation. (a) Public-sector "5018 compliant" strategic plan and performance programme modules sold inside municipal ERPs (TeraMIS, SaySis StarCities, Birsav SMSYS, Element, Netiket Unimetrics for universities). They focus on compliance reporting and budget links. (b) Quality and process suites with KPI or BSC modules (Bimser Ensemble, AltaSoft, rSmart, H2C Butterfly BSC). (c) HR and OKR suites (Twiser, DevOKR, AcademiaHRM, Kolay İK, Logo j-HR). (d) Consultancies that resell foreign tools (Ironman with QPR, Keysoft with Corporater) or do the work by hand. Many universities and some municipalities build their own systems. Twiser is the strongest local private-sector vendor: more than 200 organisations, 250,000+ users and a holding focus.

### Cited Findings

**Group A. Public-sector strategic plan and performance software (Law 5018)**

- **Netiket – Unimetrics Strateji Yönetim Sistemi** (mainly universities). One application covers strategic plan monitoring, corporate risk management and internal control. Units enter data every six months with evidence, through a draft, submitted, verified, approved workflow. It produces the year-end "Stratejik Plan İzleme Tablosu" in Word in the university guide's format. Indicator score is (C−A)/(B−A), weighted to the goal. Risk uses 1 to 5 probability and impact with residual risk, per the 2024 public risk guide. Internal control has 5 components, 18 standards, 79 conditions and a 226-question self-assessment. It is installed on the institution's own server, so data stays in the institution's database, with LDAP login and NhrWEB staff data. Optional AI: a SWOT summary made by a local language model on the institution's server, with no data leaving. No price. Customers are on a separate references page. A sister page says it produces a performance programme with 7 categories and 54 indicators and that "Unimetrics users do not re-enter indicator data" — [Netiket](https://www.netiket.com.tr/services/stratejik-plan-yonetim-sistemi)
- **Teracity – TeraMIS Stratejik Plan ve Proje modülü** (municipalities). It holds goals, objectives, performance targets and indicators. Projects and activities are linked to targets, split into sub-steps with dates and budgets, and shown on a neighbourhood map. Performance programme amounts can generate the next year's analytic budget. Contractor payments flow in from accounting in real time. Reports include the Strategic Plan Report, Performance Target Table and Total Resource Requirement Table. It states 5018 compliance and production in Turkey. No customers or prices are shown — [Teracity](https://teracity.com.tr/teramis/teramis_plan_proje.html)
- **SaySis – StarCities Kurumsal Süreçler Yönetimi** (e-belediye). The strategic plan is entered at area, objective and target level. The fiscal-year performance programme is built from it. Activity budgets are set in the budget system, and activity results are entered monthly or quarterly. It is integrated with accrual accounting, so costs need no re-entry. It outputs the Strategic Plan, Performance Programme and Activity Report for the council, stored to EBYS standards. It also has meeting and task management for council and committee decisions. No AI, no named customers on the page, deployment not stated — [SaySis](http://www.saysis.com/cozum-kurumsal-surec)
- **Birsav – SMSYS (Stratejik Mali Süreç Yönetimi)**. It "binds strategic plan, performance programme, budget and activity report into one live data chain" and claims 5018 compliance. It is part of a municipal suite (BirsavMis, Birİhale, Vatandaş 360, decision support), with AI in other modules but not in SMSYS. Logos shown: Ataşehir, Gaziantep, Kocaeli, Eskişehir, Antalya, Mersin and Sakarya metropolitan municipalities, and Trabzon. It claims "100% domestic software". Quote within 24 hours, no price — [Birsav](https://birsav.com/kamu-yazilimlari)
- **Element Bilgisayar – Stratejik Plan ve Performans Esaslı Bütçe Sistemleri** (portal for strategy departments of public administrations). Staff costs are allocated to activities with economic codes down to 5 levels. Expense data comes in by hand, from e-budget Excel exports or via web services. It has role-based access, a full audit history, login with the T.C. ID number, and web plus mobile access. No customers named — [Element Bilgisayar](https://www.elementbilgisayar.com.tr/elementyazilimlar-Stratejik-Plan-ve-Performans-Esasli-Butce-Sistemleri)
- **Olgu Bilgisayar**: Yıldırım Municipality's performance programme says its municipal automation software is bought as a service from Olgu Bilgisayar Sistemleri, and that a strategic plan and performance software was planned (2018–2019 document) — [Yıldırım Belediyesi 2019 Performans Programı](https://www.yildirim.bel.tr/tema/belediye/uploads/reports/2019_1.pdf)

## Q4. White space and the most dangerous competitor for the first ICP

### Takeaway
The white space is a Turkish-language product that starts before the strategy: an evidence and readiness gate, then structured choice with explicit trade-offs and a decision record, then execution limited by resource permits. It also needs a credible "data in Turkey or AI off" option at a mid-market price. No global or local software covers this chain. For a Turkish family firm with 50 to 499 staff facing one bounded decision, the most dangerous competitor is not a software vendor. It is "generic AI assistant plus a local (possibly KOSGEB-funded) consultant". Among vendors, Twiser is the most dangerous locally because of its brand logos, holding focus and training channel. StrategyFrame.AI is the most dangerous model to watch, because it already sells this exact job to mid-sized firms in Germany.

### Cited Findings
- Execution vendors assume an existing strategy (ClearPoint target statement) — [ClearPoint](https://www.clearpointstrategy.com/pricing)
- StrategyFrame.AI sells analysis, options, team decision and an OKR link to firms of 50 to 5,000 staff, positioned as an alternative to consultants, with coaches. It is in German and English only on its site, with no Turkey presence found — [StrategyFrame.AI](https://www.strategyframe.ai/en); [The Innovator](https://theinnovator.news/?p=11868)
- Generic AI is cheap in Turkey: ChatGPT Plus is 499 TL per month and Go is 249 TL (October 2026 press, conflicting figures exist) — [Politikam](https://www.politikam.com/chatgpt-turkiye-abonelik-fiyatlari-aciklandi-en-pahali-chatgpt-pro-500-paketi-aylik-26499-tl-oldu-iste-tum-fiyatlar). Copilot Business is about USD 21 per user per month — [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/copilot-pricing)
- KOSGEB funds consultancy including institutionalisation and family constitutions (SEGEM, capacity support) — [KOSGEB SEGEM](https://webdosya.kosgeb.gov.tr/Content/Upload/Dosya/SEGEM/2025.12.20/SEGEM_Destek_Program%C4%B1_Uygulama_Esaslar%C4%B1.pdf); [Alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/08/24/kobi-kapasite-destegi-basvurulari-basladi/)
- Boutique consultancies bundle strategic planning with family constitutions and succession planning for family firms — [Ortak Akıl](https://www.ortakakildanismanlik.com/service/stratejik-planlama-danismanligi/); [Smart&Start](https://smartandstart.com.tr/hizmetlerimiz/stratejik-danismanlik/aile-sirketi-danismanligi/)
- Twiser claims 200+ organisations and 250,000+ users, shows Turkish brand logos (Beko, Ülker, Togg, Akbank, Migros One), targets holdings and group companies, adds AI OKR quality control and coaching, and runs OKR certificate training — [Twiser](https://twiser.com/tr/); [Twiser OKR eğitimi](https://twiser.com/tr/okr-hizmetlerimiz/okr-egitimi/)
- DevOKR already markets an "AI OKR Generator" that drafts OKRs "from your strategic context" in Turkish — [DevOKR](https://devokr.com/en/blog/best-okr-software-comparison)
- Decision-record products exist but only for the enterprise and government (Cloverpop, Decision Lens) — [Cloverpop](https://www.cloverpop.com/); [Decision Lens](https://www.decisionlens.com)
- Local precedent for on-premise local AI: Unimetrics generates its SWOT summary with a local model on the institution's server — [Netiket](https://www.netiket.com.tr/services/stratejik-plan-yonetim-sistemi)

### Inferences
- **White space, in order of emptiness**: (1) a readiness gate with an evidence ladder: no product found. (2) A choice log with trade-offs and named decision owner: only enterprise or government tools (Cloverpop, Decision Lens, 1000minds), none in Turkish. (3) Resource permits tied to chosen moves: EPM tools and public 5018 modules have budget links, but none ties spending to a strategic choice for a private mid-sized firm. (4) Turkish language plus data in Turkey plus formulation: no vendor combines the three.
- **Why "AI plus consultant" is the most dangerous for the first ICP**: the trigger is one bounded decision, not a yearly management system. Family firms can get a fast answer from ChatGPT or Copilot at low cost, and a trusted adviser (possibly subsidised) for legitimacy. The product must win on "a record that stays in the company", gates, and an evidence trail, and should recruit consultants as a channel rather than fight them.
- **Why Twiser is the most dangerous vendor in Turkey**: it already sells to the holdings and group companies that sit above many target family firms. It has executive-facing AI and a training and consulting channel. Adding a "strategy" lens on top of OKRs would be a short step. Its weakness for this ICP is that it is HR-led (buyer: HR) rather than CEO-led, and it has no formulation or choice workflow on its site.
- **Why StrategyFrame.AI is the model to watch**: it proves willingness to pay for "do your own strategy with AI plus a coach" in the same company size band. If it localises to Turkish or partners with a Turkish consultancy, it would compete head-on. No evidence of that exists today.
- **Public-sector vendors** (TeraMIS, SaySis, Birsav, Element, Unimetrics) are not a threat to the first ICP. They will be the incumbents in the later public offer, where the product should complement them (preparation, evidence, choice) and export to their formats.

### Gaps
- No Turkish buyer interviews or win-loss data confirm which alternative family firms actually pick. The ranking above is inference.
- Consultant fees for a one-decision engagement in Turkey were not found.
- StrategyFrame.AI's price and expansion plans beyond the US were not found.
- Twiser's price, and whether it plans a strategy-formulation module, are unknown.
