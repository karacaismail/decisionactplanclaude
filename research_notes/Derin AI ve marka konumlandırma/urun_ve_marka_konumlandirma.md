# Product and brand positioning for Karar Atlası: competitor messaging, Turkish category evidence, name checks

Research date: 16 November 2026 (Monday). Homepages were fetched on this date. This file extends, and does not repeat, the earlier notes in `reports/Hedef kitle ICP ve AI konumlandırma.md`, `reports/Global ve yerel rakipler.md` and `reports/OKR yazılımlarından farkı.md`. Nothing here is legal advice. Trademark and domain observations are a public-record snapshot, not clearance.

**Most important single finding:** the name "Karar Atlası" is already in public commercial use in Turkey, on `kararatlasi.com.tr`, with the ® symbol, for a paid decision-making methodology. Its messaging also overlaps with this product's core promise. See question 4.

## 1. Competitor messaging teardown (as of 16 November 2026)

### Takeaway
Global suites now sell **speed and AI agents** ("AI speed", "10X faster", "in seconds") and promise execution or alignment. Turkish suites sell **HR/performance, OKR or quality-system scope** and show logos and customer counts. Almost nobody sells **defensibility of one decision** (why it was chosen, what was rejected, who owns it). The phrase "your team decides" appears only at StrategyFrame.AI, and the same idea appears on the unrelated Turkish "Karar Atlası®" site. The messaging white space is "slower, explained, owned decisions" for an owner-run mid-size firm, in Turkish.

### Inferences
- **Speed is the shared claim.** WorkBoard ("AI Speed"), Cloverpop ("10X faster"), Cascade ("faster, smarter decisions", "in seconds") and ClearPoint (AI plan from a form) all promise faster outcomes. A Turkish family-firm owner facing one large irreversible decision is not short of speed. He is short of a defensible, shared basis. Counter-positioning against speed ("hız değil, gerekçe") is open.
- **"AI decides less" is almost unclaimed.** Only StrategyFrame.AI says "your team decides". Everyone else markets agents that "plan, decide" (Insider One uses this exact pattern in its own space). The product rule "Stratej yolu önerir… Karar sahibi seçer ve kaydeder" is a real message differentiator, but StrategyFrame.AI already owns the English wording. A Turkish-language claim is still open.
- **Nobody talks to the owner.** Global suites name executives, strategy officers, PMOs, government. Twiser names holdings and group companies. No vendor names "patron", "aile şirketi", "yönetim kurulu" or "ikinci kuşak". The buyer identity itself is white space.
- **Nobody sells "one decision".** All vendors sell an always-on system (platform, OS, cycle). The ICP trigger is one bounded, money-bearing decision. A message built around "bu yatırım kararı" rather than "strateji yönetimi" is unclaimed.
- **Category labels are converging** on "strategic planning & execution" (ClearPoint, AchieveIt), "strategy management" (Spider, Netiket), "strategy execution / performance" (WorkBoard, Cascade). "Decision intelligence" is the label of the decision tools (Cloverpop, StrategyFrame.AI) and is entering Turkish press as "karar zekâsı". Cascade's move from "strategy execution" to "Strategy-Led Performance" suggests the execution label is saturated.
- **Proof style differs by market.** Global sites use outcome numbers (30%, $20M, 89 hours) and enterprise logos. Turkish sites use counts (200+, 1.300+, 4.500+) and Turkish brand logos. A new entrant has neither, so early proof must be process proof (a worked example of one decision file) and named pilots.

### Gaps
- Bimser homepage text could only be partly extracted. Headline, proof points and AI claims were not captured.
- Homepages are the vendors' own claims. Numbers like "30%" or "10X" were not verified.
- No win/loss or buyer-interview data shows which message actually moves Turkish buyers.

## 2. Category: what Turkish buyers search and say

### Takeaway
Google Trends (Türkiye, last 5 years, accessed 16 November 2026) shows "iş planı" and "okr" as the only terms with steady volume. "Stratejik planlama" is smaller, informational ("nedir") and regionally skewed toward Anatolian provinces with universities and public bodies. "Kurumsallaşma", "aile anayasası" and "strateji yönetimi" are near zero. Software-suffixed terms ("…yazılımı") did not render at all. **Turkish buyers do not search for this category as software.** The category label must work in a sales conversation and a proposal, not in search.

### Inferences
- **"Stratejik planlama" reads as a public-sector and academic word in Turkey.** The regional pattern (Amasya, Karabük, Çorum, Düzce: university and public-body towns) and the "nedir" queries suggest students and public officials, not company owners. This is an inference from Trends only.
- **"OKR" is the only management-software term with business-region search (İstanbul, Kocaeli).** But earlier reports showed the OKR price anchor is USD 6–11 per user. Using "OKR" as the category would capture search and lose price and differentiation.
- **"İş planı" has the biggest volume** but likely means founders, KOSGEB and grant applications (inference). It fits the free entry path for founders, not the paid ICP.
- **"Kurumsallaşma" and "aile anayasası" have almost no search** but are the owner's real vocabulary in press and consultant offers (question 6). These words belong in sales conversations, events and referral channels (consultants, chambers, family-business associations), not SEO.
- **Recommended category handling (inference):** keep "strateji planlama yazılımı" as the formal frame of reference in proposals, procurement and KOSGEB-style paperwork, because it is the recognised budget line. In the headline, lead with the decision ("büyük yatırım kararı", "önemli şirket kararı") rather than the category. Do not lead with "karar zekâsı": it is an enterprise analytics term and is being claimed by others.
- Budget familiarity is a separate question from search. Search shows awareness, not budget. Budget evidence remains the consultant and workshop spend from the earlier report.

### Gaps
- No absolute monthly search volumes. Trends shows only relative interest.
- Trends data for software-suffixed terms did not render. It could not be determined whether this was low volume or rate limiting; a direct Trends API call returned HTTP 429.
- "okr" may include unrelated searches; related queries suggest it is mostly the method.
- No Turkish buyer survey on which category label they budget under was found.

## 3. Positioning method applied, and Turkish B2B SaaS examples

### Takeaway
Successful Turkish B2B SaaS homepages do not lead with technology. They lead with **a plain category, local compliance (e-Fatura, mevzuat, KVKK), data in Türkiye, a short free trial, TL prices and counts of Turkish customers**. The one Turkish company selling globally (Insider) does the opposite: it hides its origin and leads with analyst badges and AI. For Karar Atlası, Dunford's steps point to "the defensible big decision for an owner-run firm" as the value, and to local trust as table stakes.

**Dunford's steps, applied (extends the earlier table; earlier steps not repeated)**
- Dunford: positioning defines how a product leads at delivering something a well-defined set of customers cares a lot about; order is alternatives → differentiated capabilities → value → target customers → market category; relevant trends are an optional final layer — [April Dunford quickstart](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning).
- Keller: frame of reference, points of parity, points of difference; past differentiators become parity over time — [Kellogg School](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research/detail/2002/three-questions-you-need-to-ask-about-your-brand/).

### Inferences
**Dunford steps for the first ICP (one bounded, money-bearing decision in a 50–499 employee family firm):**

1. *Competitive alternatives for that one decision:* the owner decides alone after informal talks; a consultant report plus a generic AI chat; an Excel feasibility from the finance manager or the bank's credit analyst; a board/family meeting with slides; postponing. (Bank and finance-manager feasibility is an added hypothesis, not sourced.)
2. *Unique attributes:* readiness gate before strategy work; versioned evidence; options with written rationale proposed by the stratej; the owner chooses and the choice is recorded with rejected options; a separate resource permit before money moves.
3. *Value:* the decision can be defended later, to the family, the board, the bank and the next generation; money is released only after the decision is recorded; the reasoning survives a consultant leaving or a generation change.
4. *Best-fit customers:* owner-run firms where (a) one decision is large relative to the balance sheet (new plant, export market, acquisition, closing a line), and (b) more than one family member or a professional manager must accept it.
5. *Market category:* "strateji planlama yazılımı" as the formal frame; lead message on "önemli bir şirket kararı".
6. *Relevant trends:* AI agents everywhere (speed claims commoditise AI); KVKK generative-AI guidance (earlier report); succession pressure (question 6).

**Moore statement draft (internal only, Turkish):**
> Tek ve büyük bir şirket kararının önünde duran, 50 ile 499 çalışanlı aile şirketleri için hazırlandı. Bu şirketler kararı bugün patronun deneyimi, Excel ve danışman raporuyla verir. Karar Atlası bir strateji planlama yazılımıdır. Stratej seçenekleri gerekçesiyle önerir. Karar sahibi seçer ve kaydeder. Para, karar kaydedildikten sonra ayrı bir izinle çıkar. Genel yapay zekâ araçları ve yabancı strateji yazılımları hız vaat eder. Karar Atlası, kararın neden verildiğini ailenin ve yönetimin önünde savunulur kılar.

**Keller update (only what changes from the earlier report):**
- New points of parity set by Turkish SaaS leaders: data stored in Türkiye (Kolay İK), KVKK statement on the homepage, ISO 27001 badge, 14–15 day free trial without card (Paraşüt, Kolay İK, Ideasoft), TL prices with VAT (Ideasoft), phone/WhatsApp support (Logo), public ratings including Şikayetvar (Ideasoft). Missing these will read as "foreign" or "unfinished".
- Points of difference that no competitor homepage claims: rejected options recorded; resource permit after the decision; readiness gate; owner as named decision-maker; a Turkish-language "AI proposes, people decide" claim.
- Insider shows the opposite path: a Turkish company competing globally hides origin. For a Turkish-first ICP, local origin is an asset; for later global sales, the brand may need a neutral English form (see question 4).

### Gaps
- No public evidence on which Turkish SaaS messages converted better (no A/B or win/loss data).
- Bordro Plus could not be accessed.

## 4. Brand: naming, trademark and domain checks for "Karar Atlası"

### Takeaway
**"Karar Atlası" has a live conflict.** `kararatlasi.com.tr` was registered on 1 August 2026, and a site titled "Karar Atlası®" has run on it since 2 August 2026. The site sells a decision methodology for personal life decisions, and its sub-brands are "Karar Netliği®", "Karar Haritası®" and "Karar Stratejisi®". Its core message is close to this product's: it gives no ready answers, and the decision stays with the person. `kararatlasi.com` and `kararatlasi.net` appear unregistered. TÜRKPATENT and TMview/EUIPO could not be queried by machine. A trademark attorney must check before any further spend on the name. This is not legal clearance.

- TÜRKPATENT's public trademark search page contains a CAPTCHA and e-Devlet login options, so it was not queried (bypassing CAPTCHAs is outside the rules for this research) — [TÜRKPATENT araştırma](https://www.turkpatent.gov.tr/arastirma-yap?form=trademark).
- TMview's search API (which aggregates EUIPO and many national offices) returned "Request Rejected"; EUIPO eSearch is a JavaScript application and was not queried — [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/).
- Nice classes: practitioner guides place downloadable software in class 9 and SaaS/cloud software in class 42, and recommend both where both forms exist; "software" alone is usually too vague and the function must be stated — [Trademark Factory](https://trademarkfactory.com/blog/saas-trademark-classes-class-9-vs-class-42/); [calcguru](https://calcguru.in/?p=5119); Nice term "Software as a service [SaaS]" in class 42 — [TRAMA class 42](https://www.tramatm.com/class-assist/trademark-class/42/software-as-a-service-saas). Class 35 (business management/consulting services) is commonly added for management software; this was not confirmed from an official TÜRKPATENT source in this research.
- A 2022 legal article reports that many Turkish applicants use TÜRKPATENT's own goods/services list (dated 2017) rather than the current Nice text — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/article/1314816).
- Earlier naming criteria (≤3 syllables, few Turkish-only letters, not a generic word; check meanings in customer languages; domain check during naming) — [Adapte Dijital](https://adaptedijital.com/?p=73517), [Spaceship](https://www.spaceship.com/blog/saas-product-name-guide/).

### Inferences
- **Risk is high, not only legal but also positioning.** The other "Karar Atlası®" sells decision guidance in Turkish, priced in TL, with "the decision is yours" messaging. A buyer searching the name will find a personal-life decision coach with mythology archetypes. That undermines a B2B "serious decision record" brand even if the classes differ (the other business is likely in education/counselling classes such as 41 or 45; this is an assumption).
- The ® symbol suggests a registration or at least a filing. Whether it is registered, and in which classes, can only be confirmed in TÜRKPATENT. Misuse of ® is also possible. A trademark attorney should check identical and similar marks containing "KARAR" in classes 9, 35, 41, 42 and 45.
- The product's own UI vocabulary ("Karar Haritası", "Karar Stratejisi" style labels) should also be checked, since the other site uses them with ®.
- **Naming criteria (Turkish and English) for a replacement or a modified name:**
  1. Owner-friendly Turkish meaning tied to *record, reason, continuity*, not to *control* (see question 6).
  2. ≤3 syllables if possible; at most one of ç, ş, ğ, ı, ö, ü (the "ı" in "Atlası" breaks ASCII domains and URLs: `kararatlasi` vs spoken "atlası").
  3. Pronounceable by English speakers without diacritics; no negative meaning in English, German, Arabic (earlier criterion).
  4. Not a generic category word ("Karar", "Strateji" alone are weak marks).
  5. `.com` and `.com.tr` free, or a clean `get…`/`…app` pattern.
  6. Distinct from "decision intelligence" brands (Cloverpop, StrategyFrame) and from Turkish "Karar" uses (newspaper, patent agency, the existing Karar Atlası®).
- If the owner wants to keep "Karar Atlası", the steps are: attorney search in TÜRKPATENT and EUIPO; ask whether the other user has filed; assess coexistence or acquisition; secure `kararatlasi.com` immediately only if the attorney sees a path (buying it alone does not solve the conflict).

### Gaps
- TÜRKPATENT and EUIPO records were not seen. Registration status, classes and filing dates of the existing "Karar Atlası®" are unknown.
- `.io` and `.ai` availability unknown.
- Whether the existing site's operator is connected to the product owner is unknown (no reference to "Sekoya" was found in the project folder).

## 5. Trust signals Turkish mid-size firm owners need before buying

### Takeaway
The trust bar is set by the local SaaS leaders the owner already uses: **data kept in Türkiye, a visible KVKK statement, ISO 27001, a free trial or demo, Turkish phone/WhatsApp support, TL prices, Turkish customer logos and public ratings (including Şikayetvar)**. Strategy-software competitors in Turkey mostly fail to show KVKK or data location, which is an opening.

### Cited Findings
- Kolay İK shows ISO 27001/27701/9001, a KVKK data-processor statement, storage "Türkiye'deki yüksek güvenlikli sunucularda", a 15-day trial without card, a free demo and weekday support — [Kolay İK](https://kolayik.com/).
- Paraşüt shows e-Fatura/e-Arşiv, 14-day free trial, expert data migration, 4.6/5 with 2,097 reviews, a KVKK notice link (no data location) — [Paraşüt](https://www.parasut.com/).
- Ideasoft shows 7/24 support, a named advisor, TL prices with VAT, Google rating and Şikayetvar 91/100 — [Ideasoft](https://www.ideasoft.com.tr/).
- Logo shows partner network (1,000+), phone and WhatsApp sales, large Turkish customer stories — [Logo](https://www.logo.com.tr/).
- Twiser shows SSO, "Denetime Hazır", demo and big-brand logos, but no KVKK, data location, ISO or trial on its homepage — [Twiser](https://twiser.com/tr/). DevOKR offers self-hosting and a free start, no KVKK mention — [DevOKR](https://devokr.com/). AltaSoft shows years in business, 1,300+ firms, demo, no KVKK — [AltaSoft](https://altasoft.com.tr/). Netiket sells "data stays in the institution" with a local language model — [Netiket](https://www.netiket.com.tr/services/stratejik-plan-yonetim-sistemi).

### Inferences
- Minimum homepage trust set for Karar Atlası (or its renamed brand): "Verileriniz Türkiye'de saklanır" (only if true), KVKK statement and DPA, ISO 27001 roadmap, AI on/off per customer (from earlier report), a priced pilot for one decision, Turkish phone/WhatsApp, TL prices.
- For a decision record, trust also means **who can read it**. Owners will ask whether a professional manager, a sibling or the consultant can see the file. Role-based visibility should be shown, not only claimed (inference).
- e-Fatura matters for billing the customer (the vendor must issue e-invoices), not as a product feature (inference).
- A pilot on one live decision is the natural proof unit and matches the ICP trigger. Free trials of 14–15 days are the local norm for self-serve tools, but a strategy decision takes longer; a paid, time-boxed pilot is more credible (inference).

## 6. Messaging for the family-business owner: sensitive words

### Takeaway
Turkish family-business writing frames resistance as **loss of the founder's authority**, not as dislike of software. Founders see the firm as their child ("evlat"), which slows delegation. A family constitution fails when the boss fills it with his own rules. Generational tension, not technology, stalls decisions. Therefore "kayıt" can be read as *control over the patron*. It should be framed as the owner's own memory and legacy ("gerekçe", "devir", "hafıza"), with the owner as the author, not the subject. No study directly tests which words owners reject; this is a gap to close with interviews.

### Cited Findings
- A columnist argues that founders view the business as "evlat", which creates resistance to delegating to professionals, and proposes written rules (entry conditions, pay, profit distribution, management handover) in a family constitution — [MuhasebeTR](https://www.muhasebetr.com/yazarlarimiz/sedatiskara/002).
- An Ekonomi Gazetesi column (13 August 2025, per fetch) says a family constitution dictated by the boss defeats its purpose ("kendi krallığının buyruklarıyla doldurmuşsanız"), should be built by consensus from the family's own culture, and must be able to restrict the author's own authority; "Uyulmayan bir aile anayasası, yok hükmündedir." — [Ekonomi Gazetesi](https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/kose-yazisi/sirketlerin-zor-secimi-kurumsallasma-mi-tek-adam-mi-14212).
- A related Ekonomi Gazetesi column defines the family constitution as rules for growth, institutionalisation and survival, and says about 20% of the process is professional support and 80% must come from the family's own culture rather than copied texts or consultant imposition (search summary) — [Ekonomi Gazetesi](https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/kose-yazisi/kotu-yonetimi-aile-anayasasi-kurtarabilir-mi-23425).
- Forbes Türkiye (4 February 2026): based on 601 SME managers, digital transformation is slowed mainly by intergenerational tension that locks decision-making ("kuşaklar arası gerilimlerin karar alma süreçlerini kilitlemesi"); full figures are paywalled — [Forbes Türkiye](https://www.forbes.com.tr/dergi/kurucular-temkinli-ikinci-kusak-hizli-dijital-donusum-gerilim-yaratiyor).
- Para Dergi (18 August 2026), citing a Deloitte global survey: 35% name the next generation's lack of experience and leadership competence as the top obstacle to succession; 33% cite difficulty finding a successor. It cites an İstanbul Ticaret Üniversitesi study that about two-thirds of family firms do not complete the transfer to the second generation, and most have no formal succession plan (sample sizes not given). Headline framing: "Veliaht doğulur, halef yetişir." — [Para Dergi](https://www.paradergi.com.tr/life-style/2026/08/18/aile-sirketlerinde-gelecek-dugumu-veliaht-var-lider-yok/amp).
- PwC 12th Global Family Business Survey (1,325 owners/leaders, 60+ countries, published 27 November 2025): fully developed succession plans for all key roles fell from 26% to 22%; 13% use AI/ML across a significant part of operations; 71% see value in hiring outside professionals for future leadership roles — [Bloomberg HT](https://www.bloomberght.com/pwc-aile-sirketleri-artan-kuresel-baskilarla-karsi-karsiya-3762476); [PwC Türkiye page](https://www.pwc.com.tr/pwc-12-aile-sirketleri-arastirmasi) (403 on direct fetch; figures via press).
- PwC 2023 Turkey findings (search summary): most Turkish family-business boards have no non-family or cross-sector members; 35% say trust is high between family members working in the firm and those who do not — [PwC 11th survey, Türkiye](https://www.pwc.com.tr/pwc-11-kuresel-aile-sirketleri-arastirmasi).
- Para Dergi (July 2023): of 200 family firms surveyed, only half have institutionalised (earlier report) — [Para Dergi](https://www.paradergi.com.tr/is-dunyasi-kulis/2023/07/24/aile-sirketlerinin-yalnizca-yarisi-kurumsallasiyor).
- Paraşüt addresses its customers as "patron" ("Mutlu Patronlarımız") — [Paraşüt](https://www.parasut.com/). Twiser uses "Denetime Hazır" as a positive enterprise claim, aimed at holdings, not owners — [Twiser](https://twiser.com/tr/).

### Inferences
- **Words likely to trigger the control fear (to test, not proven):** "denetim", "kontrol", "izleme", "hesap verebilirlik", "şeffaflık" (when it implies the patron is exposed), "uyum", "kayıt altına alma" (sounds like surveillance), "yapay zekâ karar verir".
- **Words likely to work (to test):** "kararın gerekçesi", "şirketin hafızası", "devir" / "bir sonraki kuşağa devir", "aile ve yönetim aynı bilgiyle karar verir", "son söz sizde", "kararınızı savunun", "danışman gider, gerekçe kalır".
- **Frame the record as the owner's asset.** The owner is the author and the named decision-maker. The record protects his reasoning when he is not in the room, and it is what the successor inherits. This turns "kayıt" from a check on the patron into his legacy. It fits "Veliaht doğulur, halef yetişir": the record is how a successor is trained on real decisions.
- **Do not sell "kurumsallaşma" as the product.** Owners hear it everywhere, search for it rarely (question 2), and often resist it. Use it as context ("kurumsallaşmanın ilk somut adımı: bir büyük kararı birlikte ve yazılı vermek").
- **"Aile anayasası" is adjacent, not core.** It is a consultant/lawyer deliverable. The product can store the decision rules that the family agreed, but claiming to be an "aile anayasası" tool would put it into legal-document territory.
- **Two audiences, one message.** The founder hears "son söz sizde, gerekçeniz kalıcı". The second generation hears "kararlar artık konuşulabilir ve görünür". Both are true of the same feature set. Test both in interviews.
- **Brand promise check.** "Kararın gerekçesi kaybolmaz." still fits this evidence and avoids control words. A variant that names the owner may land better: "Son söz sizde. Gerekçesi şirkette kalır." (draft, untested).

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed Turkish study measuring owners' reactions to specific words (kontrol, kayıt, şeffaflık) was found.
- Forbes Türkiye 601-manager study figures are paywalled.
- PwC Türkiye 2025 Turkey-specific breakdown could not be read (HTTP 403).
- TAİDER (Türkiye Aile İşletmeleri Derneği) materials on family protocols did not appear in search.
