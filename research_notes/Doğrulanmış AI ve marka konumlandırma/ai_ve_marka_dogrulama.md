## Which flagged items are verified, corrected or still open? (verification table)

### Takeaway
Of 48 checked items, 35 are verified from primary or official text, 10 are corrected (some materially), 1 is only partly verified (the Apple iOS version), and 2 remain blocked (trademark records and Nice class 35), with substitutes given. The most important corrections: (1) the PwC "55% of Turkish companies saw no AI gain" figure is the global figure; the Türkiye figure is 66% no revenue change; (2) the KPMG 71% and 79% figures are global, not Türkiye; (3) the KVKK workplace and agentic AI documents carry "Şubat 2026" on the cover but were published on 5 and 12 March 2026, so both dates are right for different things; (4) Gemma 4 on-device download is 2.0–2.6 GB, not 1.3 GB, and Gemma 4 was released on 31 March 2026, not 2 April; (5) the KVKK documents are recommendations ("dikkate alınabilecek hususlar"), not binding duties; the binding duties come from Law 6698 and its transfer regulation; (6) the recommended production models (Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5) are not on the Vectara leaderboard at all.

## EU AI Act after the Digital Omnibus: dates, Article 50 and transition

### Takeaway
Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (OJ L, 24.7.2026) entered into force on 27 July 2026. It moves Annex III high-risk duties to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028, leaves Article 50(1)–(6) unchanged, and gives only generative systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 until 2 December 2026 to meet Article 50(2) marking. A Karar Atlası AI feature launched now has no grace period.

### Cited Findings
- Title, date, OJ reference and entry into force (Art. 4: third day after publication) — [OJ copy of Reg. 2026/1744, p. 1 and final article](https://www.ibf-solutions.com/fileadmin/dateidownloads/regulation-2026-1744.pdf); identical Council text [PE-CONS 30/1/26 REV 1](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/PE-30-2026-REV-1/en/pdf) (signed Strasbourg, 8 July 2026; Parliament position 16 June 2026 per footnote 4).
- New Art. 113(c): 2 Dec 2027 (Art. 6(2), Annex III) and 2 Aug 2028 (Art. 6(1), Annex I); new Art. 113(a): two new Art. 5 prohibitions apply from 2 Dec 2026; new Art. 113(d): Arts. 102–110 apply from entry into force — Reg. 2026/1744 Art. 1 point (40).
- New Art. 111(2): high-risk systems on the market before the Chapter III date are covered only if significantly changed; public-authority systems must comply by 2 August 2030 — point (39)(a); recital 39 says the date of the first unit of a type and model decides.
- New Art. 111(4): pre-2 August 2026 generative systems comply with Art. 50(2) by 2 December 2026 — point (39)(b); recital 38 "transitional period of four months".
- Art. 50(7) replaced: Commission assesses codes of practice on marking and labelling; may adopt an implementing act if inadequate — point (20); recital 41.
- Art. 4 AI literacy: "shall take measures to support"; no specific level required — point (5).
- Annex III not amended; Annexes I and VIII amended — points (41)–(42).
- Art. 50 text: 50(2) exception for "assistive function for standard editing" or no substantial alteration; 50(4) exemption for human-reviewed text under editorial responsibility; 50(5) information at the latest at first interaction, clear and distinguishable, accessible — [AI Act Art. 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/).

### Inferences

### Takeaway
